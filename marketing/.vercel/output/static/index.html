Frequently asked questions

How does the AI generate chase sequences?
ChaseAI uses Google Gemini AI to analyze your invoice details — client name, amount, days overdue, and your preferred tone — then generates a multi-step email sequence with perfectly escalating messaging. Each email is unique and contextual.
Will automated reminders damage my client relationships?
The opposite. Our AI crafts professional, context-aware messages that maintain your relationship while getting results. You can review and edit every email before it sends. Most clients appreciate the clear, professional communication.
Can I use my own AI API key?
Yes. ChaseAI supports Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) — add your Google Gemini API key in Settings to get unlimited AI sequence generation on Starter, Pro, and Enterprise.
What happens after I generate a sequence?
You get to review every email in the sequence before anything sends. Edit subjects, body text, or send dates. When you're happy, click Start Smart Chasing and emails fire automatically on your schedule.
Do you offer Enterprise?
Yes. Enterprise builds on Pro with integrations and workflows we scope together—accounting tools, mail automation, and onboarding are planned after you contact us. Pricing is custom.
