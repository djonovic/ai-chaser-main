Frequently asked questions

How does ChaseAI generate chase sequences?
It reads your invoice details (client name, amount, overdue period) and writes a series of follow-up emails that escalate naturally. You can edit everything before it sends.
Will it damage my client relationships?
No. This is the first thing we designed for. Every message is professional and warm. The tone protects the relationship while still getting you paid.
How are emails sent?
ChaseAI sends follow-up emails on your behalf using your display name. You review and approve every message before it sends. Clients see your name in the From line.
What happens after I upload an invoice?
ChaseAI reads it and generates a full follow-up sequence in seconds. You review, approve, and it handles the rest.
