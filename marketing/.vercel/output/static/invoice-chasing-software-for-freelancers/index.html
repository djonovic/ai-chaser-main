The real cost of late payments (time + cashflow)

Freelancers do not just lose money when invoices are late. They lose momentum. Manual follow-up means rewriting the same reminders, tracking who replied, and guessing whether to be friendly or firm.

That admin overhead quietly eats billable hours and delays cashflow planning. Good invoice follow up automation removes that burden by giving you a repeatable system.

How ChaseAI works (PDF -> sequence -> automate)

  1. Upload invoice PDF (or add details manually).
  2. AI extracts client name, amount, and due date.
  3. Generate a full reminder sequence with tone progression.
  4. Edit drafts and set your preferred schedule.
  5. Activate automation and monitor responses.

You can test this flow immediately in the overdue invoice sequence generator.

Features that matter (tone, open tracking, pause/stop)

  • Tone progression: friendly -> direct -> firm without sounding rude.
  • Open tracking: see which reminders are opened so you escalate with context.
  • Pause/stop controls: stop reminders instantly when payment lands.
  • PDF extraction: avoid manual copy/paste from invoice fields.
  • Editable drafts: every message is customizable before sending.
