Cookie Policy

Last updated: March 16, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Global Bridge MNE, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on the ChaseAI website at chaseai.app and the ChaseAI application (collectively, the "Service").

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, understand how you use the site, and improve your experience. Similar technologies include pixels, local storage, and session storage.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

2.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the Service to function. They cannot be disabled.

Cookie Provider Purpose Duration
sb-*-auth-token Supabase Authentication session management Session / 1 year
sb-*-auth-token-code-verifier Supabase PKCE authentication flow Session

2.2 Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website so we can improve it.

Cookie Provider Purpose Duration
_ga Google Analytics Distinguishes unique users 2 years
_ga_* Google Analytics Maintains session state 2 years
va_* Vercel Analytics Privacy-friendly page view analytics Session

2.3 Functional Cookies

These cookies remember your preferences for a better experience.

Cookie Provider Purpose Duration
theme ChaseAI Stores your light/dark mode preference 1 year

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services we use:

  • Google Analytics: Provides website usage statistics. Google may use the data collected to contextualize and personalize ads in its own advertising network. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel Analytics: Provides privacy-friendly, aggregate web analytics without collecting personal data. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.

4. Managing Cookies

4.1 Browser Settings

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Here are links to instructions for common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Note: Blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent the Service from functioning correctly.

4.2 Google Analytics Opt-Out

You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4.3 Do Not Track

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. ChaseAI currently does not respond to DNT signals, but we respect your browser's cookie settings.

5. Cookie Consent (EEA/UK Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we will ask for your consent before placing non-essential cookies (analytics and functional cookies) on your device. You can change your cookie preferences at any time by clearing your cookies and revisiting the site.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
