We believe getting paid shouldn't be hard

ChaseAI exists because no one should spend hours writing uncomfortable payment follow-up emails. We built the tool we wished existed — AI that handles the chase, so you can focus on the work.

The story behind ChaseAI

Every freelancer knows the feeling. You delivered great work, sent the invoice, and then... silence. Days pass. Then weeks. You write a polite follow-up. Nothing. You write another one, this time a bit firmer. Still nothing. Now it's awkward.

The worst part isn't the money (though that hurts). It's the emotional cost. The time spent writing and rewriting emails. The anxiety of not knowing if you're being too pushy or too passive. The damage to client relationships when things get tense.

We built ChaseAI to solve this exact problem. Our AI writes the emails you'd want to send — professional, contextual, perfectly escalating — so you never have to. You review, approve, and the system handles the rest.

What we stand for

The principles that guide every feature we build.

🎯

Freelancers first

We build for the solo operator — the designer, developer, consultant, or agency owner who does it all. Every feature is designed for people who don't have an accounting department.

🤖

AI that actually helps

We don't use AI as a buzzword. Our AI generates genuinely useful, contextual emails that you'd be proud to send. Every message is unique, not a template with variables swapped in.

🔓

Transparent & fair

No hidden fees on self-serve plans, no surprise charges, no vendor lock-in. Bring your own API key if you want. We earn your business by being useful — and Enterprise customers get a team that ships integrations with them.

⚡

Simple over complex

We don't try to be an ERP or a full accounting suite. We do one thing — chase invoices — and we do it exceptionally well. Setup takes 2 minutes, not 2 hours.
