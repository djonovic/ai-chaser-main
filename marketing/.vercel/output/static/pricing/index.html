Pricing FAQ

What counts as an "AI sequence generation"?
Each time you click Generate Smart Sequence on an invoice, that counts as one generation. You can regenerate or create new sequences within your monthly limit. Pro includes unlimited generations (or unlimited on any plan when you add your own Gemini API key in Settings).
Can I use my own API key to bypass AI limits?
Yes. Add your Google Gemini API key in Settings and you get unlimited AI sequence generations on Starter, Pro, and Enterprise. ChaseAI adds zero markup to API costs.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, and PayPal. All payments are processed securely through Stripe.
Can I switch plans at any time?
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade between Starter and Pro anytime from your Settings page. When upgrading, you get immediate access to the new plan features. When downgrading, the change applies at the end of your current billing period. Enterprise is arranged separately — contact us.
Is there a contract or commitment?
Starter and Pro are month-to-month or annual, with no long-term contract. Cancel anytime with one click — no cancellation fees. Enterprise terms are agreed when you sign up with our team.
Do you offer refunds?
Yes. If you're not satisfied within the first 14 days of a paid Starter or Pro subscription, contact us for a full refund. No questions asked.
What's included in Enterprise?
Enterprise builds on Pro with integrations and workflows we define together after you reach out—accounting tools, mail automation, onboarding, and support. Pricing is custom; we scope it on a short call or over email.
What's included in Priority Support?
Priority support means faster response times (under 4 hours vs 24 hours), direct access to our team, and priority handling of feature requests and bug reports.
