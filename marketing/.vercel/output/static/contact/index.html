Before you reach out

Quick answers to common questions.

How fast do you respond?
We aim to respond within 24 hours for all users. Starter and Pro users get priority support with response times under 4 hours during business hours.
I forgot my password — how do I reset it?
Go to the login page and click "Forgot password" or use our Magic Link option. You'll receive a password reset email within minutes.
Can I request a feature?
Absolutely! We love hearing from users. Use the contact form above and select "Feature Request" — every request is reviewed by our team.
Do you offer enterprise plans?
Yes. Enterprise builds on Pro with integrations and workflows we scope after we understand your stack—accounting tools, mail automation, and onboarding are discussed on the call. Use the form above and select "Enterprise Inquiry."
