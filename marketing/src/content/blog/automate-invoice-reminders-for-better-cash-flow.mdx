## Step 2: Set Up Your Invoice Reminder Sequence

Timing is everything. Use this proven 4-stage sequence:

### Stage 1: Initial Invoice (Day 0)
Send immediately after project completion.

**Template**:
```
Subject: Invoice [INV-001] - Thank You for Your Business!

Hi [Client Name],

Thanks for the great project! Attached is your invoice for $1,250 due on [Due Date].

[Invoice Link]

Questions? Reply here.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### Stage 2: Gentle Reminder (Day 7)
For early nudges.

**Template**:
```
Subject: Quick Check-In: Invoice [INV-001] Due Soon

Hi [Client Name],

Just a friendly reminder about your invoice [INV-001] ($1,250) due on [Due Date].

[Payment Link]

Let me know if there's an issue.

Thanks,
[Your Name]
```

### Stage 3: Firm Reminder (Due Date + 3 Days)
Escalate politely.

**Template**:
```
Subject: Payment Overdue: Invoice [INV-001]

Hi [Client Name],

Your invoice [INV-001] ($1,250) is now overdue by 3 days.

[Payment Link]

Please remit payment or let me know the status.

Regards,
[Your Name]
```

### Stage 4: Final Notice (Due Date + 14 Days)
Include late fees if applicable.

**Template**:
```
Subject: Urgent: Overdue Invoice [INV-001] + Late Fee

Hi [Client Name],

Invoice [INV-001] ($1,250 + $62.50 late fee) remains unpaid.

[Payment Link]

Further delays may pause services. Settle today.

[Your Name]
```

Copy-paste these into your tool. Customize with client data.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Too Aggressive Early**: Wait 7 days for first reminder.
2. **No Personalization**: Use [Client Name] always.
3. **Forgetting Attachments**: Embed payment links.
4. **Ignoring Responses**: Pause sequence on replies.
5. **No Late Fees**: State policy upfront (e.g., 5% after 30 days).

**Legal Note**: Include terms in contracts: "Invoices due Net 15. Late fee 5% monthly."
