## Core AR Best Practices: Set Up for Success from Day One

Start with foundational habits that minimize late payments before they happen.

### 1. Ironclad Contracts and Payment Terms

Never begin work without a signed agreement. Include:

- **Clear scope and deliverables**
- **Payment milestones** (e.g., 50% upfront, 50% on delivery)
- **Net-15 or Net-30 terms** maximum—avoid Net-60
- **Late fees**: 1.5% per month, starting day 31
- **Dispute resolution process**

**Template Example**:

Payment Terms: Net-15. 50% deposit required. Late payments incur 1.5% monthly fee. All disputes resolved via email within 7 days.

### 2. Upfront Deposits: Non-Negotiable Rule

Require 30-50% upfront for all projects over $1,000. This:

- Filters serious clients
- Funds initial work
- Reduces your risk exposure

Pro Tip: Frame it positively—"This secures your spot in my schedule and funds custom research."

### 3. Professional Invoicing Standards

Ditch free invoice generators. Use tools with:

- **Branded templates**
- **Payment links** (Stripe, PayPal)
- **Due date prominence**
- **Line-item breakdowns**

Send invoices immediately upon milestone completion.

## Advanced Cash Flow Optimization Tactics

Beyond basics, layer these for **freelancer cash flow optimization** mastery.

### Dynamic Pricing for High-Risk Clients

Assess client history:
- New clients: Net-15 + 50% deposit
- Repeat payers: Net-30
- Agencies: Net-45 max, with credit check

### Diversify Payment Methods

Offer:
- Credit cards (3% fee pass-through)
- ACH transfers (free/fast)
- Crypto (for tech clients)
- Installment plans via Klarna/Affirm

### AR Aging Reports: Weekly Ritual

Track buckets:
- Current (0-30 days)
- 31-60 days
- 61+ days

Aim for <5% in overdue buckets. Use Excel or integrate with QuickBooks.

**Simple Aging Template**:

| Client | Amount | Days Overdue | Status |
|--------|--------|--------------|--------|
| ABC Corp | $2,500 | 45 | Escalated |
| XYZ Ltd | $1,200 | 12 | Reminder sent |

### Incentive Structures

- **Early bird discount**: 2% off for payment within 7 days
- **Volume bonuses**: Bundle discounts for annual retainers
- **Referral rewards**: Free hours for on-time payers who refer

## Legal AR Best Practices: When to Get Tough

60% of freelancers avoid legal action due to fear. Don't.

### Key Protections

- **Signed contracts** = enforceable
- **Small claims court**: Up to $10K in most states, no lawyer needed
- **Mechanics liens** for service work
- **Collection agencies**: 25% commission, but 90% recovery

Document everything: emails, calls, delivery proofs.
